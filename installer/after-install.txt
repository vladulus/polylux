Two BIOS settings Polylux needs (BIOS Advanced Mode, F7):

1. Monitor > Q-Fan Configuration > Q-Fan Control = Disabled
   on every fan header you want Polylux to control.

   With Q-Fan on, the motherboard firmware keeps overwriting the fan
   chip behind Polylux's back, and your fan curves won't hold. The fans
   run at full speed from power-on until Polylux starts, then follow
   your curves.

2. Tool > ASUS Armoury Crate > Download & Install ARMOURY CRATE app
   = Disabled.

   Otherwise Windows reinstalls Armoury Crate by itself on the next boot.

After changing them, press F10 to save and reboot.
